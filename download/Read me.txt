LowStim offline desktop edition

Extract the entire LowStim folder. Open the launcher named for your computer.
The launcher opens the slate in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:41731.
Keep the launcher running while using LowStim. Close its window to stop.
Windows and Mac builds are unsigned. On Linux, allow executing the launcher
if your archive tool did not retain its executable bit.

Draw on the paper. Double-tap or hold to type, speak, or open the menu.
Open Offline in the slate menu and enable Qwen and Whisper. The complete ZIP
includes both model files, so this step also works with Wi-Fi off. The smaller
app-only ZIP downloads these files on first setup. Drawing and text also work
before model setup. Use the same browser and profile to keep models and saves.
Sign in once online to use your LowStim identity. Saved creations in this
edition stay on this device. Open the hosted website to use your cloud library.
The app never needs to send drawings, voice recordings, or prompts to a server.

For Pixel, open the hosted site in Chrome and choose offline setup from the
slate menu. Install both models, then use the browser's Install app option.
